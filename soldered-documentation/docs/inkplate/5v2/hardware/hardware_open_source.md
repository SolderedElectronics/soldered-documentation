Inkplate is a crowdfunded, open-source project, and its software libraries build on other free, community-driven libraries. Open source made Inkplate possible, so at [**Soldered Electronics**](https://soldered.com/about-us/) we share back the knowledge and resources we've picked up along the way.

<CenteredImage src="/img/inkplate_6_motion/open_source_hardware.png" alt="Open source logo" />

Soldered designs and manufactures electronic products that help makers build their ideas. Everything is developed in-house in Osijek, Croatia, following open-source principles, and we work with makers of all experience levels.

<InfoBox>Need help? Our team offers free technical support, so if you run into trouble with your Inkplate or any other electronics project, [**reach out**](https://soldered.com/contact/).</InfoBox>

All designs in the Inkplate 5V2 repository are distributed without warranty and provided "AS IS". We put care into our work, but we can't guarantee these resources will fit every use case. Using, modifying, or implementing these designs is your responsibility. For details, check the [**LICENSE**](https://github.com/SolderedElectronics/Soldered-Inkplate-5-Gen2-hardware-design/blob/main/LICENSE.md) files in our repositories.
