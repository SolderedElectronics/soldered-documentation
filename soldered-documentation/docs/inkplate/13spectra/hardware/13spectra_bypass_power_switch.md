The Inkplate 13SPECTRA can be configured to power on automatically when current is present, bypassing the power switch.

<CenteredImage src="/img/13spectra/power_bypass.jpg" alt="R14 bypass pads location highlighted on Inkplate 13SPECTRA" caption="R14 location" width="700px" />

To bypass the power switch, simply short the **R14** pads by directly adding a small amount of solder, or soldering a 0Ω resistor.
